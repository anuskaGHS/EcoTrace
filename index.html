Awareness and Education

How to Segregate Your Waste

Simple guidelines for every household in Bangalore. Proper segregation is the first step toward a cleaner city.

Wet Waste

Green Bin
  • Food scraps and leftover food
  • Vegetable and fruit peels
  • Garden waste and leaves
  • Cooked food and eggshells
  • Tea bags and coffee grounds

Dry Waste

Blue Bin
  • Paper, cardboard, newspapers
  • Plastic bottles and packaging
  • Glass bottles and jars
  • Metal cans and foil
  • Cloth and textile scraps

Hazardous Waste

Special Disposal
  • Batteries and cells
  • Paints and chemicals
  • Medicines and syringes
  • Pesticides and fertilisers
  • Aerosol cans

E-Waste

E-Waste Centres Only
  • Old mobile phones and laptops
  • Cables, chargers, remotes
  • CFL bulbs and tube lights
  • Printers and televisions
  • Batteries and power banks

5 Practical Tips to Reduce Household Waste

1
Use cloth bags instead of single-use plastic bags when shopping.
2
Start a small compost bin at home for wet waste — it becomes natural fertiliser.
3
Buy products with minimal packaging and prefer reusable containers.
4
Donate old clothes, furniture, and electronics instead of throwing them away.
5
Never mix wet and dry waste — always use separate bins and hand over to BBMP collectors.
